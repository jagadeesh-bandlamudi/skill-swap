Features

Everything you need to grow

A complete toolkit for peer-to-peer learning — from discovery to live video sessions.

Smart Discovery

Find partners by skill, category, or name — with recommendations matched to what you want to learn.

Skill Swaps

Send a request, connect, and agree to teach each other. No fees, ever — just a fair exchange of value.

Live Video Sessions

Meet face to face with built-in HD video calls, screen sharing, and mute controls — right in the browser.

Real-time Chat

Message your connections instantly, share files and images, and keep every conversation in one place.

Rich Profiles

Showcase the skills you offer and want to learn, set your availability, and add a profile photo.

Verified & Secure

Email-verified accounts and secure authentication keep the community trusted and safe.
